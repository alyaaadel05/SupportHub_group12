--------------------------------------------------------------------------

    messages were published to the isolated topic by that probe in session 1 (recorded in 02).
  - Floci does not log SNS deliveries; subscriber delivery is evidenced by a temporary
    verification-only SQS subscriber (removed) and by Lambda receipts.
  - Lambda failure recovery relies on platform async retries (Floci retried once after 60 s);
    no on-failure destination/DLQ and no automated reconciliation of published events without
    a receipt (the gap is detectable by SQL, see 06).
  - The Lambda gets DATABASE_URL as a plain environment variable (real AWS: Secrets Manager/KMS).
  - Re-review keeps the article published and searchable (current Article state machine has no
    published->outdated transition); duplicate reporters' reason text is not stored.
  - The worker's SNS client has no explicit request timeout (not changed).
  - Requeue of failed SNS events is an operator/scheduler call; no scheduler is configured.
  - Migration 008 is applied ONLY to the isolated database. It must be applied (npm run
    db:migrate with the documented Neon safety gate) BEFORE the new flagService.js is deployed
    anywhere else - this is a manual step for the team.
